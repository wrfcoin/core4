## 3. USE CASES AND STAKEHOLDER BENEFITS

Wrfcoin creates value for diverse stakeholders through its unique combination of environmental
utility and cryptographic innovation. The protocol serves multiple interconnected use cases that
collectively form a robust ecosystem.

### Individual Participants

#### Earn Through Environmental Contribution

Individuals can participate in the Wrfcoin network in several ways, each providing direct economic
rewards:

1. **Data Collection Contributors**

   - Deploy low-cost environmental sensors and submit data to the network
   - Earn higher rewards for data from underrepresented geographic regions
   - Maintain privacy while contributing valuable environmental information
   - Build reputation through consistent, accurate submissions

2. **Forecasting Contributors**

   - Develop and publish forecasting models using network data
   - Receive rewards proportional to model accuracy and uniqueness
   - Improve models over time to increase reward potential
   - Gain recognition for specialized expertise in environmental domains

3. **Network Infrastructure Providers**

   - Run validator nodes to secure the network and verify submissions
   - Operate storage nodes to maintain the distributed environmental database
   - Deploy computation nodes to process complex forecasting tasks
   - Supply bandwidth and relay services to ensure global network access

4. **Offsetters and Environmental Stewards**
   - Earn WRF by documenting verifiable carbon reduction activities
   - Receive rewards for reforestation and habitat restoration projects
   - Implement renewable energy systems with tokenized benefits
   - Create and maintain biodiversity monitoring programs

#### Privacy-Preserving Value Storage and Transfer

For individuals concerned about financial privacy and censorship resistance, Wrfcoin offers:

- Private transactions that protect user financial activity
- Self-custody without intermediaries or permission requirements
- Cross-border value transfer without traditional banking limitations
- Protection against surveillance and financial censorship
- Sound money attributes including scarcity and divisibility
