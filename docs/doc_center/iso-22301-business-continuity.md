# ISO 22301 - Business Continuity Management Compliance

**Date Created:** May 26, 2025  
**Last Edited:** May 26, 2025

## Overview

Wrfcoin implements comprehensive Business Continuity Management System (BCMS) in accordance with ISO
22301:2019 to ensure uninterrupted weather data services and blockchain operations. This framework
protects critical weather forecasting infrastructure and maintains service availability during
disruptive events.
