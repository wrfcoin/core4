# ISO 14064 - Greenhouse Gas Emissions Compliance

**Date Created:** May 26, 2025  
**Last Edited:** May 26, 2025

## Overview

Wrfcoin implements comprehensive greenhouse gas (GHG) accounting and verification in accordance with
the ISO 14064 series standards. This framework ensures transparent, accurate, and verifiable carbon
accounting for our blockchain operations while supporting global climate monitoring and carbon
market integrity.

## Compliance Framework

### ISO 14064-1: GHG Quantification and Reporting

✅ **Compliant**: Comprehensive organizational GHG inventory

- **Scope 1 emissions**: Direct emissions from blockchain mining operations
- **Scope 2 emissions**: Indirect emissions from purchased electricity
- **Scope 3 emissions**: Value chain emissions including validator nodes
- **Boundary setting**: Clear organizational and operational boundaries
- **Quantification methodologies**: IPCC-aligned calculation methods

#### Direct Emissions (Scope 1)

✅ **Compliant**: Mining and validation operations monitoring

- **Proof-of-Work calculations**: Energy consumption from consensus mechanisms
- **Facility operations**: Data center backup generators and heating systems
- **Transportation**: Business travel and equipment transportation
- **Real-time monitoring**: Continuous emission tracking and reporting

#### Indirect Emissions (Scope 2)

✅ **Compliant**: Electricity consumption tracking

- **Grid emission factors**: Location-based and market-based approaches
- **Renewable energy certificates**: Green energy procurement tracking
- **Validator node consumption**: Distributed network energy monitoring
- **Data center efficiency**: Power Usage Effectiveness (PUE) optimization

#### Value Chain Emissions (Scope 3)

✅ **Compliant**: Comprehensive supply chain assessment

- **Hardware manufacturing**: Mining equipment lifecycle emissions
- **Network participants**: Validator and node operator emissions
- **User transactions**: End-user device energy consumption
- **Waste management**: Equipment disposal and recycling emissions
