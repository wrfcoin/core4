# ISO Standards Compliance Overview

**Date Created:** May 26, 2025  
**Last Edited:** May 26, 2025

## Overview

Wrfcoin maintains comprehensive compliance with multiple ISO (International Organization for
Standardization) standards to ensure the highest levels of security, privacy, quality, and
interoperability across our weather data blockchain platform. This document provides an overview of
our compliance framework and implementation status.

## Implemented ISO Standards

### Security and Privacy Standards

#### ISO 27001 - Information Security Management

✅ **Fully Compliant**

- Comprehensive Information Security Management System (ISMS)
- Advanced cryptographic controls and access management
- 24/7 security monitoring and incident response
- Annual third-party security audits

#### ISO 27701 - Privacy Information Management

✅ **Fully Compliant**

- Privacy Information Management System (PIMS)
- Complete data subject rights implementation
- GDPR, CCPA, and international privacy law alignment
- Advanced privacy-enhancing technologies

### Financial and Business Standards

#### ISO 20022 - Financial Services Messaging

✅ **Fully Compliant**

- Universal Financial Industry Message Scheme implementation
- CBDC and traditional banking integration
- Cross-border payment support
- Weather data marketplace payment processing

#### ISO 4217 - Currency Codes

✅ **Fully Compliant**

- Complete international currency code support
- Cryptocurrency and digital asset integration
- Multi-currency weather data pricing
- Real-time exchange rate processing

#### ISO 22301 - Business Continuity Management

✅ **Fully Compliant**

- Comprehensive Business Continuity Management System (BCMS)
- Weather service resilience and disaster recovery
- Crisis management and emergency response
- Regular continuity testing and validation
