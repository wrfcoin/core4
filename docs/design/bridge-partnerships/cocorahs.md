# CoCoRaHS volunteers

**Value hypothesis to validate:** continuity of careful local precipitation
observations and useful community science. The attempted official
[about page](https://www.cocorahs.org/Content.aspx?page=aboutus) returned 403 to
the research reader on 2026-10-28. Current participation and reuse terms are
therefore unverified; do not imply an agreement or automate access.

**Likely objections to ask about:** replacing established reporting routines,
confusing manual observations with automated station readings, reward framing,
and requests to share data beyond the observer's authority.

**Who to approach after approval:** program/state coordinators and consenting
adult observers. First ask whether this project adds useful context without
changing their observation protocol. Do not scrape observer records.

**Offer:** a reference-only comparison and data-quality discussion, preserving
all existing submissions. There is no CoCoRaHS connector or ownership proof in
this implementation; importing public records cannot establish a claim.

**Data terms:** obtain current program terms and explicit permission before any
reuse. Public availability is insufficient. This is not an immediate earning or
automated-ingest pilot. Reconsider priority only after protocol fit and rights
are established; measure retained voluntary participation and additional
independent coverage, not the number of copied public records.
