# Vineyards

This brief is a hypothesis for discovery, not a claim about a named vineyard.

**Value hypothesis:** consistent local observations and documented sensor
quality. **Likely objections to ask about:** commercially sensitive location or
crop conditions, proprietary account terms, ongoing support and unreliable
experimental data. Do not offer frost, crop-loss or insurance guarantees.

**Who to approach after approval:** the station owner and the person authorized
to approve data sharing. Clarify contractor versus vineyard ownership and any
shared weather-service account before attempting proof.

**Offer:** voluntary dual publication, quality status and a clear withdrawal
path. Keep existing monitoring and alert destinations. Testnet participation
carries no promise of token value or mainnet carry-over.

**Data terms:** explicit consent for the observations, location granularity,
retention and downstream uses. A publicly accessible feed is not a licence to
redistribute it or claim its hardware. MADIS is a separate reviewed destination.

**Priority and metrics:** discovery after the initial pilots. Reuse the
agricultural pilot's independent sparse-cell coverage, 30/90-day retention and
ownership-dispute measures. Distinguish added independent instruments from
multiple feeds of the same hardware.
