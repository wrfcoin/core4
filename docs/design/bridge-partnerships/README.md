# Station community partnership plan (draft)

Refs wrfcoin/core4-archive#3572 and wrfcoin/core4-archive#3568.
This is a research and pilot proposal. No outreach, affiliation, spending or
pilot activation is authorized by it. Community priorities below are hypotheses
to validate with their representatives, not statements that they endorse WRFCoin.

Offer: keep existing destinations, demonstrate transparent station ownership
and quality status, and evaluate additional coverage. Testnet tokens carry no
promised value; make no money, mainnet carry-over or NOAA acceptance claims.
Current bridge proof is fixture-only, real email delivery needs a later owner go
within the zero budget, and vendor redistribution terms are unverified.

## Proposed shortlist

| Priority | Proposed pilot | Why first | Proposed success measures |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | A US amateur-radio club with voluntarily participating station owners | Exercises the owner-ratified licence-email path with technical participants | At least 80% of enrolled owners still reporting at day 30, 60% at day 90; report upheld disputes per 1,000 claims and time to resolve |
| 2 | A school/university station program with an adult institutional custodian | Clear equipment steward and educational use, with reviewable metadata | Sustained observations in at least one previously unserved paid cell at day 30; day-90 retention and QC pass rate |
| 3 | An agricultural cooperative with member-owned stations | Tests sparse-cell coverage and shared-account boundaries | At least two additional sparse paid cells with independent observations; day-30/day-90 retention and duplicate-feed holds |

Targets are proposals for small pilot cohorts, not commitments or measured
results. For small samples publish raw numerator/denominator alongside rates.
Existing community membership never substitutes for station ownership proof.

Pilot start requires source-specific owner verification, terms/redistribution
clearance, a live-proof delivery decision within budget, participant consent,
clear dispute ownership and support capacity. Non-US APRS remains reference-only.
Preserve direct CWOP publishing; any MADIS integration needs a separately agreed
provider process and duplicate-destination handling. No vendor rights are inferred
from public readability. Do not copy community member lists or contact emails.

existing destinations must remain; what data may be redistributed; how should

with consent and publish only agreed aggregate findings. Owner approval is
required before contacting anyone or identifying an actual pilot organization.
