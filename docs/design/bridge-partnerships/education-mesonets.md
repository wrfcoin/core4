# Schools, universities, GLOBE and state mesonets

[GLOBE Weather](https://www.globe.gov/do-globe/resources/teaching-resources/globe-weather)
describes school weather investigations and observation protocols; the
[GLOBE Atmosphere program](https://www.globe.gov/web/atmosphere/) covers multiple
weather variables. Sources checked 2026-10-28. These facts motivate exploration,
not a claim that GLOBE or a mesonet authorizes WRFCoin data reuse.

**Value hypothesis:** teachable provenance, repeatable QC and maintained
instrument metadata. **Likely objections to ask about:** student privacy,
maintenance burden, curriculum mismatch and misleading financial incentives.

**Who to approach after approval:** an adult institutional equipment custodian,
science educator or mesonet data steward. A student account or public dashboard
is not proof of institutional authority. Do not collect student identities.

**Offer:** an opt-in, institution-controlled comparison station with transparent
quality history and unchanged existing publication. Keep location disclosure
and participation decisions with the institution.

**Data terms:** each institution/network must approve sharing and retention.
Do not infer redistribution rights from educational purpose or public portals.
Pilot metrics: additional independent coverage in paid sparse cells, QC
completeness and day-30/day-90 retention. Confirm support ownership over school
breaks before starting. No project affiliation or reward value is promised.
