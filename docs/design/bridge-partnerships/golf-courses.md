# Golf-course operators

This brief is a hypothesis for discovery, not a claim about a particular course.

**Value hypothesis:** understandable local weather records and low maintenance.
**Likely objections to ask about:** another account to maintain, proprietary
station-service terms, public location attribution and confusion with official
lightning or severe-weather warning systems. This is not a safety alert service.

**Who to approach after approval:** equipment/data custodian and course
management with authority over the station account. A contractor's read access
or a public course station identifier is not ownership proof.

**Offer:** optional dual publication and a quality/status view that leaves all
existing services intact. Do not promise operational savings or financial
returns. Prefer a single consented station before considering wider deployment.

**Data terms:** verify both hardware ownership and permission under any
maintenance/vendor contract. No downstream reuse without an explicit agreement;
MADIS opt-in remains separate. Do not expose personal contact information.

**Priority and metrics:** later than the first three pilots, unless an owner
volunteers with already-cleared rights and an independent sparse-cell location.
Track retention, new coverage and dispute resolution using the shared measures.
