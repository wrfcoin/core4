# Agricultural cooperatives

This brief is a hypothesis for discovery, not a claim about any named operator.

**Value hypothesis:** local coverage and understandable station reliability.
**Likely objections to ask about:** farm-location disclosure, vendor-account
sharing, workload, competitive data use and dependence on an experimental app.
Do not position observations as validated agronomic or safety advice.

**Who to approach after approval:** cooperative technical/data staff and the
individual equipment owner. Establish whether the cooperative or member owns
the device and who controls the vendor account; access alone proves neither.

**Offer:** a small dual-publish pilot with clear opt-out, quality status and
ownership dispute handling. Existing destinations and contractual data services
remain unchanged. Avoid promising improved yields, savings or token value.

**Data terms:** review each vendor contract and the cooperative's authority to
share member observations. Precise locations and commercially sensitive data
need explicit consent. MADIS forwarding is a separate opt-in and rights check.

**Metrics:** independent coverage added to at least two sparse paid cells,
30/90-day retention, duplicate holds and upheld disputes. Reject copied feeds
and correlated same-operator aliases from the independent-coverage count.
This is proposed pilot three, after identity and rights review.
