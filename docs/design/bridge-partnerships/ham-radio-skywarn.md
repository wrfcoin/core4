# Amateur radio clubs and SKYWARN participants

The [NWS SKYWARN program](https://www.weather.gov/skywarn/) concerns trained
volunteer severe-weather reporting. Do not equate that role with ownership of a
fixed weather station or imply an NWS affiliation. [NWS CWOP guidance](https://www.weather.gov/pub/JoinCWOP)
separately describes contributing station observations through CWOP to MADIS.
Sources checked 2026-10-28.

**Value hypothesis:** useful local observations, technical transparency and
preserving established reporting paths. **Likely objections to ask about:**
commercial-token framing, attribution, duplicate reports, volunteer support load
and the risk that a callsign is mistaken for hardware ownership.

**Who to approach after approval:** club technical/education coordinators and
participating station owners; ask a local SKYWARN coordinator about appropriate
boundaries, not endorsement. No names, addresses or contact list are collected.

**Offer:** a small, opt-in ownership/quality walkthrough that keeps current CWOP
publishing intact. Explain US licence-email proof, stale-email risk and the
non-US reference-only limit. A passcode or status packet is insufficient.

**Data terms:** owner consent plus explicit destination/redistribution review.
Do not infer NOAA rights or acceptance. Do not rebroadcast RF or transmit APRS
packets from the project. Use the shared shortlist's day-30/day-90 retention and
dispute-resolution metrics; stop the pilot if ownership disputes cannot be held.
